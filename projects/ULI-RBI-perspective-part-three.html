NPCI Perspective – Unified Lending Interface (ULI)

Although the Reserve Bank of India initiated and is the regulatory backbone of this concept of ULI via their RBI innovation hub, it does not directly involve in the ULI project. It is conceptualized and developed by NPCI.
Just as NPCI governs the UPI payments ecosystem, it will oversee the end‑to‑end loan‑flow lifecycle: onboarding borrowers and lenders, certifying schemes, monitoring transactions, and using AI/ML to anticipate macro‑economic trends.

“NPCI’s role in ULI is to provide trust, stability, and foresight for a nation‑wide credit‑distribution rail.”

1️⃣ NPCI Core Mandates in ULI

NPCI’s responsibilities span the entire credit‑distribution lifecycle:

  1. Borrower onboarding, verification & regulation – KYC, credit‑score validation, eligibility checks.
  2. Lender onboarding, verification & regulation – licensing, FCO list enforcement, compliance monitoring.
  3. Government‑scheme registry & central portal – creation, versioning, and public exposure of subsidised‑loan schemes.
  4. Regional admin dashboards – real‑time monitoring of transaction volumes, risk exposure, and macro‑economic impact across states and districts.

2️⃣ Borrower On‑boarding, Verification & Regulation

2.1 KYC & Document Validation

Borrowers upload required documents which flow through the Central Document Validation Service (CDVS) before NPCI issues a Borrower‑Verified Token (BVT).

2.3 Ongoing Monitoring

NPCI mandates periodic re‑KYC, AML screening, and black‑list updates. Any change triggers a re‑issuance of the BVT.

3️⃣ Lender On‑boarding, Verification & Regulation

3.1 Licensing Verification

Lenders submit licence numbers, capital adequacy proofs, and insurance documents. NPCI validates against its master registry.

3.2 Field‑Collection Officer (FCO) Registry Enforcement

NPCI audits the FCO list quarterly to ensure on‑ground verification capability.

3.3 Ongoing Supervision

NPCI monitors transaction‑level data, enforces NPA thresholds, and requires periodic stress‑tests.

4️⃣ Government‑Scheme Registry & Management Portal

4.1 Central Scheme Catalog

A version‑controlled repository stores all loan‑subsidy schemes, eligibility rules, and subsidy percentages.

4.2 CRUD API for Schemes

  • POST /admin/schemes/create
  • PUT /admin/schemes/{schemeId}
  • DELETE /admin/schemes/{schemeId}
  • GET /admin/schemes (audit log included)

4.3 Public Scheme Explorer

Lenders and borrowers can query /api/v1/schemes to discover applicable schemes.

5️⃣ Regional Admin & Monitoring Dashboards

5.1 Dashboard Tiles

  • Heat‑Map of transaction volume by state/district.
  • Lender Risk Exposure per region.
  • Borrower Credit‑Score Distribution.
  • Scheme Uptake heat‑map.

5.2 Alert Engine

Threshold‑based alerts (e.g., sudden spike in loan approvals in a district) are pushed to regional admins via email/SMS.

5.3 Drill‑down Views

Clicking a state reveals lender‑wise breakdown, NPA trends, and scheme performance.

8️⃣ Compliance & Reporting Framework

  • Real‑time Auditing – Every API call is logged with an immutable hash (SHA‑256) stored in an append‑only ledger.
  • Periodic Reports – Daily, weekly, and monthly compliance packs (PDF/CSV) automatically generated and delivered to NPCI’s internal portal.
  • Regulatory Sandbox – NPCI can spin up a sandbox environment for new schemes or policy experiments without affecting production.
